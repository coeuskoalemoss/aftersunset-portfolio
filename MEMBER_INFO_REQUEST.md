# aftersunset — info needed for your artist profile

We're putting a proper profile for each of us on the website (photo, short bio, credits).
**Copy the block below, fill in your own, and send it back in the group.**
Rough notes are totally fine — I'll tidy up the wording. Don't stress about making it sound polished.

---

## Copy this and fill it in 👇

```
NAME:            (how you want it shown on the site, e.g. Shreyas)

INSTRUMENT/ROLE: (e.g. Guitar & Vocals, Bass, Drums, Keys, Lead Vocals)

TAGS:            (2–4 quick words for your vibe, e.g. Bollywood retro, rock, funk, ghazal)

BIO:             (2–4 sentences, first person or not — your call. Ideas to cover:
                  - how you got into music / how long you've been playing
                  - any training (classical, grades, self-taught, etc.)
                  - what you do in the band / what you bring on stage)

CREDITS:         (3–5 bullet points — anything that shows you're the real deal:
                  - other bands / projects you've played in
                  - notable shows, festivals, venues
                  - studio / session / jingle / playback work
                  - awards, competitions, qualifications
                  - how many shows / years of experience)
                  •
                  •
                  •

GEAR/SPECIALTY:  (optional — e.g. "Fender Jazz bass + in-ears", vocal range,
                  the genres you anchor, software you produce on)

LINKS:           (optional — your personal Instagram / YouTube / Spotify / SoundCloud)

PHOTO:           (send 1–2 good photos of you separately in the group.
                  Best: a clear portrait, shot vertical/portrait orientation,
                  high resolution. Don't worry about colour — the site is black & white.)
```
