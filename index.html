01 — The Band

Pune's own house band for the dance floor.

Five musicians, one packed floor, every time. aftersunset plays the hits — old Bollywood, new Bollywood, Marathi favourites and a handful of originals — arranged live for weddings, sangeets and the kind of night that only really begins once the sun is down.

We have played 150+ shows across the city — college fests, highstreet venues, private lawns and hotel ballrooms. The set is built around the room: a slow-burn opening, a peak that stays up, and songs the whole family already knows the words to.

03 — The Artists

Five players, five stories.

The full profiles behind the band — how each player got here and what they bring to the floor.

Guitar & Vocals · Bandleader

Shreyas

Bollywood retroRockArranger

Shreyas founded aftersunset in 2018 and still builds every setlist. A self-taught guitarist who grew up on R.D. Burman and classic rock in equal measure, he fronts the band on the big singalongs and keeps the room moving between songs. Off stage he arranges the covers for a five-piece and runs the band's rehearsals.

Selected Credits

  • 150+ live shows across Pune, Mumbai and Nashik
  • Former lead guitarist, The Midnight Locals (2015–2018)
  • Session guitar for regional ad jingles and short films
  • Music director, Symbiosis college productions (2017)
Instagram YouTube
Lead Vocals

Harshita

PlaybackGhazalSemi-classical

Harshita trained in Hindustani classical vocals for nine years before moving into stage and studio work. She carries the band across eras — the ghazal-soft numbers one minute, a full-throttle sangeet closer the next. Her range and control are why the band can program a set that moves from 1970s classics to this year's chart-toppers without losing the room.

Selected Credits

  • Visharad (Hindustani vocals), Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya
  • Playback and scratch vocals for independent releases
  • Finalist, regional televised singing competition (2019)
  • Lead vocalist, 120+ aftersunset shows

Bass

Sunny

FunkRetro groovesPocket

Sunny holds down the low end and the pocket. A funk and disco obsessive, he's the reason the retro floor-fillers actually make people dance — locking in with the drums and leaving just enough space for everything above him to breathe. He plays a Fender Jazz bass through in-ears and keeps the dance floor honest.

04 — On Stage

What a booking looks like.

Practical details for planners and venues. We travel light and adapt to the space — lawn, ballroom or rooftop.

Format
5-piece live band — guitar, lead vocals, bass, drums, keys. Backing tracks kept minimal.
Set length
Two 45-minute sets, or one continuous 90, with a DJ handover either side.
Languages
Hindi, Marathi, and English covers on request.
Space
Roughly 4m × 3m stage, power, and cover if outdoors. We bring our own backline and in-ears.
Sound
We can bring a compact PA for up to ~150 guests, or plug into house sound with an engineer.
Travel
Pune base. Mumbai, Nashik and nearby on a per-event basis.
